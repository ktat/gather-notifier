How to Use

  1. Reload Gather.town tabs

    Open each Gather.town tab and press Ctrl+R (Cmd+R on Mac) to reload.

  2. Configure notifications

    Click the extension icon to customize which notifications you want to receive.

  3. Stay notified

    Work in other tabs - you'll receive desktop notifications, badge alerts (! icon), and sound notifications when something happens in Gather.town!

  4. Use Concentration Mode

    Click the extension icon and toggle concentration mode to temporarily disable notifications. The badge shows "C" when active.
